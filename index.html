What ALIS is

ALIS is a private automation system operated by its owner to manage their own brand accounts across social platforms. It securely connects each account by the platform's official OAuth flow, verifies the connected account's identity, and helps schedule and publish short-form videos. Publishing is approval-gated and defaults to private visibility.

What it does

  • Authorizes only accounts the operator personally owns and controls.
  • Reads basic profile information to confirm the correct account is connected.
  • Prepares and, when explicitly approved, publishes the operator's own video content.
  • Never sells data and never posts on behalf of anyone other than the operator.
