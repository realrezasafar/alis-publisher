This Privacy Policy explains how ALIS ("the Service") handles information. ALIS is a private automation system used by its operator to manage social-media accounts the operator personally owns.

1. Information the Service accesses

When the operator connects an account through a platform's official OAuth flow, the Service receives:

  • Access and refresh tokens for the connected account, used to call the platform's API.
  • Basic profile information (such as the account's open ID, display name, username, and avatar) used solely to confirm that the correct account has been connected.

The Service requests only the minimum permissions needed to verify identity and to publish the operator's own content.

2. How information is used

Information is used only to (a) verify that the connected account matches the intended account and (b) prepare and, upon the operator's explicit approval, publish the operator's own video content. The Service does not use this information for advertising or profiling.

3. Storage and security

Tokens are stored locally on the operator's own machine in access-restricted files (owner-only, permission mode 600) and are never transmitted to third parties other than the originating platform's API. Non-secret identifiers may be recorded in a local database for verification and audit.

4. Sharing

The Service does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties. Data is exchanged only with the official APIs of the platforms the operator has connected.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens and identifiers are retained only while an account remains connected. The operator can revoke access at any time from the platform's settings and can delete the locally stored tokens, which permanently removes the Service's access to that account.

6. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age required by the connected platforms.

7. Changes

This Policy may be updated from time to time; the "Last updated" date will reflect any changes.
